* Once the visitor is added, they appear in the **Department Queue** under the selected department.

Now I want to take this one step further and make the system feel more like a real-world application.

### New requirement: Department staff notification & approval

When a receptionist registers a visitor for a particular department:

2. That person should receive a **real-time notification/popup** that a visitor is waiting for them.
3. The department person should be able to **Approve / Accept** or potentially reject the visitor.
4. Once they approve the visitor, the receptionist should immediately receive a **real-time notification** without refreshing the page.
5. The entire flow should feel seamless and real-time.
