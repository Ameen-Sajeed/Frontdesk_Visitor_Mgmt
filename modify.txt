For a reception/visitor system, there are several very practical problems you can solve.
1. The biggest problems worth solving
Problem	What usually happens	ArriVo solution
🕐 Long waiting	Visitor waits without knowing what is happening	Waiting timer + threshold intervention
👤 Host unavailable	Reception keeps calling/message the employee	Host status + quick notify
📱 Manual communication	WhatsApp/calls for every visitor	One-click notifications
🔁 Repeat visitors	Reception enters the same person repeatedly	Smart visitor recognition/autofill
❓ Where is my visitor?	Employee doesn't know visitor has arrived	Real-time arrival notification
🚶 Visitor leaves	No proper record of why/when	Left-without-meeting workflow
📝 Rejection confusion	“Rejected” without explanation	Simple rejection reason/comment
📊 Management visibility	No idea about waiting times/traffic	Simple analytics
🗂️ Finding old visits	Searching manually through records	Universal visitor history
🪪 Physical badges	Manual badge issuing/returning	Digital/QR visitor pass
📅 Appointments	Reception doesn't know expected arrivals	Appointment queue
🔔 Missed notifications	Employee misses a message	Escalation / reminder

If I were building ArriVo commercially, I wouldn't position it simply as:
